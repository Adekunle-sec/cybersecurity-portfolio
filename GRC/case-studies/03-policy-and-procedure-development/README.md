# Policy & Procedure Development

**Classification:** Supervised operational task  
**Organization:** Halisans Technologies  
**Date:** 2026-10-19  
**My role:** Information Security Policy Analyst  
**Status:** Completed

## 1. Problem and objective
Develop and review organizational access control, acceptable use, and incident response procedures aligned with regulatory frameworks.

Informal and outdated security procedures exposed the enterprise to operational inconsistencies, compliance penalties, and ambiguity during security incidents. Establishing clear, enforceable, board-approved policies is the cornerstone of defensible cybersecurity governance.

## 2. Scope, environment and authorization
- **Systems/data:** Enterprise Policy Management Repository, Confluence Governance Space, Employee Training Portal.
- **Tools:** Microsoft 365 Compliance Center, NIST SP 800-53 Rev 5 control catalog, SANS Policy Templates.
- **Authorization:** Authorized policy revision charter approved by Halisans Security Leadership.

## 3. Methodology
1. Regulatory & Threat Horizon Review: Analyzed requirements from ISO 27001:2022, NDPR, and NIST CSF 2.0 to define mandatory policy clauses.
2. Drafting & Cross-Functional Alignment: Authored formal drafts for Access Control Policy, Acceptable Use Policy (AUP), and Incident Response Plan (IRP).
3. Stakeholder Review & Executive Sign-off: Facilitated legal, HR, and IT executive reviews, resolving 18 operational feedback items before final sign-off.

## 4. Evidence
| Evidence ID | Sanitized artifact | What it proves |
|---|---|---|
| E-01 | Approved Access Control Policy Document (v3.0) | Proves formal codification of least privilege, password complexity, and mandatory MFA standards. |
| E-02 | Incident Response Playbook Flowcharts | Demonstrates clear escalation paths, RACI matrices, and containment timelines for Tier-1 to Tier-3 incidents. |
| E-03 | Employee AUP Attestation Records | Validates 98.4% workforce acknowledgment of Acceptable Use requirements. |

## 5. Findings and analysis
Previous policies lacked explicit cloud data handling definitions and remote work device security guidelines. The incident response procedure had ambiguous severity categorization criteria that delayed critical escalations.

## 6. Outcome and recommendations
- **Disposition / deliverable:** Published three ratified enterprise security policies with mandatory annual employee re-attestation.
- **Action taken or recommended:** Integrated policy attestation into the HR automated onboarding workflow and scheduled annual tabletop policy review simulations.
- **Validation:** Verified through HR LMS portal that all active employees completed policy attestation within 30 days of release.
- **Limitations:** Policy documentation sets expectations; operational effectiveness depends on continuous technical enforcement (GPO/MDM).

## 7. Lessons learned
Policies must be accompanied by concise 1-page quick-reference guides to ensure user comprehension and adherence.
